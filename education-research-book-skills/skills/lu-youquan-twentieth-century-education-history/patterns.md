## 3. Three-wave timeline

For each period record geopolitical structure, economic/technical change, dominant education problem, reform instruments, country variation, excluded region/group, and transition mechanism. Use alternate dates when the case does not fit the book's boundaries.

## 4. Context–mechanism–outcome chain

`context → actor problem definition → instrument/resources → institutional mediation → enacted practice → experienced effect → delayed outcome → feedback`

Attach evidence and a rival explanation to every arrow.
