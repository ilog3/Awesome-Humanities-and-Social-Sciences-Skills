# 分析、理论化、离场与交流

## Analysis is continuous

Analysis begins while collecting and recording data. Break material into manageable units, then reassemble it into meaningful wholes. Regularly review notes and shift effort from broad collection toward focused comparison as the study develops.

## Analytic moves

- code and annotate records;
- identify features, categories, sequences, processes, and relationships;
- compare people, events, settings, times, and negative cases;
- construct and revise typologies;
- ask the same question in different forms;
- move dialectically between fragment and whole;
- test emerging ideas with new observations and interviews.

Theoretical options include analytic induction, sensitizing concepts, constant comparison/grounded theory, existential interpretation, and other interpretive forms. Select the form that answers the question; do not force data into a favored school.

## Analytic audit

For every central claim retain: supporting episodes, disconfirming material, alternative explanations, role/access conditions, and a link to original notes. Theory is an organized interpretation of facts, not decoration added after coding.

## Leaving the field

Fieldwork rarely has an absolute natural end. Exit may follow sufficient analytic development, deadlines, resources, risk, loss of access, or negotiated commitments. Taper participation where possible, communicate appropriately, return obligations, secure records, and anticipate relief, sadness, guilt, or grief on all sides.

## Writing as analysis

Define the audience and central problem. Draft the findings core first if useful, then conclusion, introduction, and revisions. Explain entry, role, relationships, evidence, analysis, and limitations. Include enough situated evidence for readers to assess interpretation while protecting people and restricted settings.
