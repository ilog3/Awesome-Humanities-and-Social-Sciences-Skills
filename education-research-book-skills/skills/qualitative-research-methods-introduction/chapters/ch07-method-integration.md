# Qualitative–Quantitative Integration

The qualitative–quantitative divide includes assumptions about reality, knowledge, causality, values, and evidence, not only data format. Integration is justified when methods answer complementary parts of a coherent question.

## Designs

- **Exploratory sequential:** qualitative findings develop constructs, instruments, or hypotheses for later measurement.
- **Explanatory sequential:** quantitative patterns identify cases or puzzles for qualitative explanation.
- **Convergent:** collect both strands in parallel and compare or combine at interpretation.
- **Embedded:** place one strand inside the other to address process, implementation, or context.

Specify priority, timing, dependence, sampling relation, point of integration, and final inference. Do not conduct two separate studies and call their co-location integration.

When results diverge, inspect construct mismatch, sampling, timing, context, measurement, researcher influence, and genuine heterogeneity. Contradiction can improve the explanation.
