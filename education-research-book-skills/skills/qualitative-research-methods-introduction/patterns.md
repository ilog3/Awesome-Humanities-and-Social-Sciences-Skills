## Worked mini-example

Question: How do first-year teachers interpret and respond to classroom silence?

- Interpretive orientation: meaning of silence in situated interaction.
- Maximum-variation sample across subject, school, and class composition.
- Observe lessons, interview teachers and selected pupils, and examine lesson reflections.
- Build case timelines, code interpretations and responses, compare mismatches between observed silence and reported meaning.
- Seek negative cases where silence signals concentration rather than disengagement.
- Use participant feedback to challenge specific interpretations, not certify the entire account.
