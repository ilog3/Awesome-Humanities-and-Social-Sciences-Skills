# Qualitative Research Cheatsheet

## Design

- Name the phenomenon before the method.
- Align paradigm, question, relation, sample, evidence, claim, and quality test.
- Define case, setting, participant, and unit of analysis separately.
- Match purposive sampling to intended variation.
- Build ethics and rigor into the design.

## Fieldwork

- Track access and missing voices.
- Separate observation from inference.
- Ask for incidents before abstractions.
- Record interactional context.
- Memo surprises and researcher effects.
- Adapt transparently as understanding develops.

## Analysis

- Read whole cases before fragmenting.
- Compare cases and meanings.
- Develop categories beyond topics.
- Preserve negative evidence.
- Match analytic method to tradition.
- Maintain a source-to-claim trail.

## Writing and quality

- Identify audience and authorial position.
- Contextualize and interpret every major quotation.
- State claim scope and limitations.
- Match checks to actual threats.
- Treat feedback and triangulation as new evidence.
- Explain contradictions rather than hiding them.

## Red flags

- “Qualitative means no numbers.”
- “Rapport removes bias.”
- “More methods automatically improve validity.”
- “Participants confirmed it, so it is true.”
- “All sources must converge.”
- “Reflexivity means writing mainly about myself.”
