# Education-System Analysis Cheatsheet

## Fast route

1. Is this an educational activity or only an educational influence?
2. What are the educator, learner, content, and material conditions?
3. How do teaching and learning condition each other?
4. At which system layer is the decision or failure located?
5. What historical and social conditions shape it?
6. How do population, economy, governance, and culture act in both directions?
7. Which possibilities exist, and what learner activity could actualize them?
8. What happens to both social and individual-development functions?
9. Which proxy may have displaced the purpose?
10. What evidence is needed now, later, and after a long delay?

## Common correction table

| Weak analysis | System correction |
|---|---|
| “The policy says it, so classrooms do it.” | Trace translation and feedback across four layers. |
| “The teacher taught it, so students learned it.” | Analyze learner activity and developmental evidence. |
| “Environment caused the outcome.” | Identify the activity converting conditions into development. |
| “Technology improved education.” | Rebuild the four-element map and test relations and access. |
| “Education serves the economy.” | Audit both functions and education's distinctive direct object. |
| “Student choice is always developmental.” | Examine prior capacity, conditions, consequences, and teaching responsibility. |
| “This age group is naturally like this.” | Use stage claims as revisable hypotheses; assess variation and context. |
| “Scores prove the system works.” | Run the function-displacement and time-horizon tests. |

## Minimum answer

- Boundary and unit
- Four-element map
- Layered pathway and feedback
- Historical/current context
- Social-subsystem matrix
- Development mechanism
- Dual-function and proxy audit
- Multi-horizon evidence
- Leverage point, owner, risk, and revision date

## Historical caution

The book was published in 1991. Verify contemporary policy, statistics, institutions, science, and social terminology. Retain its systems questions without treating period-specific conclusions as timeless facts.
