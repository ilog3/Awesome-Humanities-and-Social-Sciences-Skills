`prior development + environmental opportunity/constraint → meaningful learner activity → interpretation and feedback → repeated participation → developmental change`

For every arrow, state the mechanism, evidence, rival explanation, and adjustable condition.

## 6. Function-displacement audit

1. Name the operative metric.
2. State the educational purpose it is meant to represent.
3. List what it measures and misses.
4. Identify adaptations or gaming it induces.
5. Identify whose interests and experience disappear.
6. Add balanced measures and decision safeguards.
