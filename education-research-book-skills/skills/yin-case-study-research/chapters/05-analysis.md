# 分析案例研究证据

## General strategies

- rely on theoretical propositions;
- work the data from the ground up;
- develop a case description;
- examine plausible rival explanations.

Use qualitative and quantitative evidence when relevant. Software can organize and retrieve data but does not supply the analytic strategy.

## Five techniques

### Pattern matching

Compare an empirically observed pattern with a predicted pattern, including non-equivalent dependent variables or rival predictions. Greater specificity makes matching more informative.

### Explanation building

Develop a causal explanation iteratively: propose, compare with findings, revise, examine other case details, and repeat. Guard against drifting away from the original question.

### Time-series analysis

Compare observed sequences or trends with theoretically predicted timing, ordering, interruption, or competing series. A chronology alone is not time-series analysis.

### Logic models

Specify a linked chain from inputs and activities through intermediate outcomes to later results. Test both the connections and alternative paths; real models can be iterative rather than strictly linear.

### Cross-case synthesis

Treat each case as a separate study, summarize case-level findings, and compare replication patterns. Use word tables or matrices to show evidence without collapsing cases into decontextualized variables.

## Analytic discipline

Attend to all evidence, address major rival interpretations, focus on the most significant aspect, use prior expertise, and show how conclusions follow. Search actively for evidence that would support an opposite conclusion.
