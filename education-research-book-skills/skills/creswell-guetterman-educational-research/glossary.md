# Glossary

- **Research problem:** Educational issue or evidence deficiency requiring systematic inquiry.
- **Purpose statement:** Concise declaration of a study's central intent, participants/site, key variables or phenomenon, and design orientation.
- **Quantitative variable:** Measurable characteristic varying across units or time.
- **Central phenomenon:** Main process, concept, or experience explored qualitatively.
- **Experimental design:** Design estimating assigned intervention effects through controlled comparison.
- **Correlational design:** Design estimating association or prediction among variables.
- **Survey design:** Design describing population characteristics or trends from sampled responses.
- **Grounded theory:** Design developing an explanatory theory of process through iterative data analysis and sampling.
- **Ethnography:** Design interpreting culture-sharing patterns through sustained fieldwork.
- **Narrative research:** Design interpreting experience through stories, temporality, context, and relation.
- **Mixed methods:** Integration of quantitative and qualitative methods and inferences.
- **Action research:** Cyclical practitioner/participant inquiry aimed at informed local improvement.
- **Convergent design:** Mixed design merging parallel quantitative and qualitative results.
- **Explanatory sequential design:** Quantitative phase followed by qualitative explanation.
- **Exploratory sequential design:** Qualitative exploration followed by quantitative development/testing.
- **Joint display:** Structure that places evidence together to support integrated interpretation.
- **Research consumer:** Reader or decision-maker evaluating and applying research.
- **Research producer:** Person or team planning, conducting, and reporting research.
