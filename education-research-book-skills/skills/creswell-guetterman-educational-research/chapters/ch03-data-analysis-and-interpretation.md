# Data Analysis and Interpretation

## Quantitative

Prepare a codebook, inspect data entry, missingness, distributions, outliers, reliability/measurement, and design structure. Describe participants and variables, then select inferential models from question, scale, design, clustering, repeated observations, and assumptions. Report estimates, uncertainty intervals, effect sizes, exact tests/models, diagnostics, multiplicity, sensitivity, and practical meaning.

Interpretation separates result from explanation and considers comparison with predictions and prior work, rival explanations, external validity, limitations, and unexpected findings.

## Qualitative

Organize and securely prepare files; read the whole; write memos; segment and code; develop descriptions, categories, themes, chronology, or process; relate and compare them; represent with narrative, matrices, diagrams, or maps; and verify against evidence.

Validate through appropriate combinations of prolonged/varied engagement, triangulation, negative cases, participant feedback, peer review, audit trail, rich description, and reflexivity. Explain what each checks and its limits.

## Shared inference ladder

`source/data → processed result → pattern/theme/estimate → interpretation → explanation → transfer/generalization → implication`

Each step requires a new warrant. Preserve source traceability and report uncertainty and contradictory evidence.
