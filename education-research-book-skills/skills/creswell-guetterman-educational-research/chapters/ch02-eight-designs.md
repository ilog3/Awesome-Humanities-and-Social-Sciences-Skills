# Eight Research Designs

## Quantitative

- **Experimental:** treatment manipulation, comparator, random assignment where possible, outcome timing, causal inference, validity threats, effect and uncertainty. Include true, quasi-, factorial, within-group/repeated, and single-subject variants only with their assumptions.
- **Correlational:** explain or predict using measured relations. Define direction/form, temporal order, confounders, range, measurement error, model assumptions, validation, and limits. Prediction does not prove cause.
- **Survey:** cross-sectional or longitudinal description of a population from a sample. Audit frame, coverage, mode, questionnaire, pilot, response/nonresponse, weights, trends, and inference.

## Qualitative

- **Grounded theory:** study a process or action; iteratively collect/analyze, code, compare, memo, sample theoretically, connect categories, and produce a bounded explanatory theory.
- **Ethnography:** study a culture-sharing group through sustained fieldwork, participation, interviews, artifacts, language, patterns, context, reflexivity, and ethical representation.
- **Narrative:** study one or a few individuals' experiences through stories, chronology, turning points, context, interaction, restorying or narrative analysis, and negotiated representation.

## Cross-family

- **Mixed methods:** collect/analyze quantitative and qualitative data and integrate them in convergent, explanatory sequential, exploratory sequential, experimental/embedded, or justified custom designs.
- **Action research:** practitioner/participant inquiry using cycles of problem focus, reconnaissance/evidence, plan, action, observation, reflection, and revision; may be practical or participatory/critical.

For any design report defining features, rationale, procedures, quality threats, ethics, and the inference it can and cannot support.
