## Worked mini-example

Question: How did a school merger affect a rural community?

- Sample former pupils, teachers, nonteaching staff, parents, local officials, and residents on different sides.
- Separate dates and administrative actions from experiences of belonging, loss, mobility, and opportunity.
- Compare interviews with minutes, enrollment data, maps, photographs, and local media.
- Analyze why the same closure is narrated as decline, liberation, efficiency, or betrayal.
- Return a catalogued local copy subject to restrictions; publish only claims supported by the sample.
