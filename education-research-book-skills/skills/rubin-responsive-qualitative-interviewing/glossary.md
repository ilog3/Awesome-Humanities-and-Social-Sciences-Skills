# Glossary

- **Accuracy** — credibility achieved through firsthand knowledge, clarification, corroboration, and faithful interpretation (Ch 3).
- **Conversational partner** — an interview participant treated as a knowledgeable relational collaborator rather than a standardized respondent (Ch 1, 4).
- **Cultural interview** — interview oriented toward shared meanings, interpretations, and rules (Ch 1).
- **Difference sampling** — selecting contrasting people or settings to test boundaries and broaden or restrict a conclusion (Ch 3).
- **Floodgate structure** — broad invitation that allows a sustained narrative with limited interruption (Ch 6).
- **Follow-up question** — question developed from emerging analysis to pursue a concept, theme, contradiction, or gap (Ch 6).
- **Main question** — planned question addressing a central domain of the research question (Ch 6).
- **Middle-range theory** — explanatory account intended for specified conditions rather than universal scope (Ch 7).
- **Probe** — in-conversation prompt used to clarify, deepen, detail, regulate, or check an answer (Ch 6).
- **Responsive interviewing** — iterative interviewing that changes questions and sampling in response to what is learned (Ch 1–2).
- **River-and-channel structure** — following the partner's conversational flow while steering into needed topical channels (Ch 6).
- **Similarity sampling** — selecting comparable cases to test recurrence of an interpretation (Ch 3).
- **Theme** — patterned assertion or account extending across parts of the material (Ch 7).
- **Thoroughness** — credibility through adequate perspectives, detail, contexts, variation, and alternatives (Ch 3).
- **Topic interview** — interview focused on a bounded event, program, process, or issue (Ch 1).
- **Transparency** — documenting selection, relationships, procedures, analysis, and limitations so conclusions can be assessed (Ch 3, 8).
- **Tree-and-branch structure** — systematic coverage of main topical branches with follow-up probes (Ch 6).
