# Glossary

- **Actual work** — Observed conduct through which the administrative role is performed, contrasted with formal prescriptions.
- **Annual cycle** — Recurring school-year rhythms that reorganize administrative attention and action.
- **Case boundedness** — Explicit delimitation of person, role, site, organization, time, and relevant context.
- **Descriptive-first stance** — Commitment to show what occurs before judging it against a normative standard.
- **Ethnographic adequacy** — Capacity of a contextualized account to make patterned conduct intelligible and support calibrated anticipation.
- **Formal contact** — Scheduled or officially defined encounter associated with administrative procedure.
- **Informal contact** — Unscheduled exchange through which information, coordination, and relationships are maintained.
- **Interval observation** — Structured recording at fixed intervals used to test time-use or interaction impressions.
- **Participant perspective** — Meaning and interpretation attributed by actors, compared across roles rather than assumed uniform.
- **Person-in-role-in-context** — Unit linking individual conduct to office, organization, relationships, and historical setting.
- **Shadowing** — Following a focal actor across time and settings to observe work as it unfolds.
- **System maintenance** — Practices that reproduce coordination, expectations, continuity, and organizational functioning.
- **Socialization by the principal** — Ways the leader inducts others into school expectations.
- **Socialization of the principal** — Ways career and organization shape the leader's role conduct.
- **Triangulation** — Comparison of observational, documentary, interview, structured, and participant evidence.
