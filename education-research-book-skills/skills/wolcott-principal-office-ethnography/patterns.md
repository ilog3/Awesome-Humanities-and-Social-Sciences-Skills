# Patterns

## Shadowing plan

**When to use:** To study work that is fragmented and mobile.

**How:** Sample whole days, different weekdays, annual phases, formal meetings, informal spaces, travel, and after-hours events. Track inaccessible settings separately.

**Trade-off:** Depth on one actor can marginalize other perspectives.

## Role-expectation comparison

**When to use:** When policy, self-report, and practice differ.

**How:** Compare prescribed duty, principal account, staff/student/parent expectation, observed conduct, and consequence. Interpret the mismatch rather than scoring compliance automatically.

## Annual-cycle map

**When to use:** To prevent one short period from standing for the job.

**How:** Plot recurring events, workload peaks, transitions, decisions, rituals, and stakeholder contacts across the school year.

## System-maintenance analysis

**When to use:** For routines that look merely administrative.

**How:** Ask what expectation, relation, boundary, information flow, or legitimacy the practice reproduces; then ask whom it benefits or excludes.
