# “深度”作为意义探究

## Two senses of depth

Distinguish:

1. a semi-structured or intensive interview format;
2. an inquiry that reconstructs how actors experience and give meaning to their life world.

The essay is concerned with the second. Drawing on phenomenological sociology, it treats depth as sensitivity to the meaning structures of everyday experience rather than simply long duration, many questions, emotional disclosure, or a large transcript.

## From material to insight

“顿悟” names an experienced researcher's apparently immediate perception of significance. Do not mystify it. Operationalize it as a trainable cycle:

`close encounter → surprise or tension → comparison with the whole case → alternative interpretations → return to evidence → provisional conceptualization`

Good intuition stays answerable to records and counter-readings. It does not license an analyst to declare hidden essence.

## Life-history orientation

Treat gestures, facial expressions, silence, rhythm, setting, and the narration itself as part of the data carrier. Reconstruct:

- the participant's natural attitude and practical concerns;
- temporal turning points and remembered relationships;
- how the story is produced in this interview relationship;
- tensions between present narration and past experience;
- what cannot be recovered from the encounter.

## Quality test

An interview has analytic depth when it can show how a situated episode organizes meaning and how that interpretation was reached. It is shallow when it merely accumulates quotations, treats vivid stories as self-explanatory, or uses “lived experience” to shield claims from examination.
