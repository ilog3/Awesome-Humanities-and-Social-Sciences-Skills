# 反身性批判

## The critique returns

An anti-discourse statement is still discourse; a critique of power—knowledge also produces power—knowledge. The analyst cannot step into a pure position outside language, institutions, disciplines, or media. Reflexivity therefore means making one's own conditions consequential to the analysis, not adding a ceremonial paragraph about bias.

## Reflexive audit

Examine:

1. **Position** — institutional authority, language repertoire, relationship to participants.
2. **Medium** — what recording and writing systems privilege.
3. **Category** — which imported and local concepts organize attention.
4. **Transformation** — who transcribed, translated, anonymized, coded, and edited.
5. **Audience** — what reviewers, disciplines, and publishers recognize as rigorous.
6. **Consequences** — whose voice, safety, reputation, or agency may change through publication.
7. **Counter-reading** — how a participant or scholar from another tradition might dispute the account.

## Productive response

The essay does not require abandoning interviews, phoneticizing Chinese, or finding a perfect recording technique. It calls for sensitivity to the pain and impossibility of representation and for practices that make universal-looking methodological institutions “困惑”—visible as contingent and revisable.

Turn critique into design:

- retain multiple representations rather than one authoritative transcript;
- annotate consequential normalization;
- let participants clarify local expressions without granting member checking absolute authority;
- preserve uncertainty and alternative readings;
- explain why this method fits this question and linguistic setting;
- state residual losses that no protocol repairs.

## Epistemic caution

The source is a theoretical, knowledge-sociological essay supported by examples and textual argument. Treat it as a framework for generating questions and redesigning method, not as empirical proof that every Western setting is speech-centered or every Chinese setting is writing-centered.
