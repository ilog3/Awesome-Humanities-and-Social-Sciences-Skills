## 2. Value–goal–function chain

Use when a broad aspiration needs to become actionable.

`Value → desired capability/relationship → observable practice → immediate evidence → longer-term function → unintended effect`

Test whether the chain balances socialization and individualization and whether evidence extends beyond compliance or grades.

## 3. Method and harm audit

For each proposed parenting practice, document:

- intended learning;
- developmental and contextual fit;
- degree of coercion;
- child voice and alternatives;
- risks to dignity, safety, privacy, attachment, and participation;
- less restrictive method;
- feedback and stopping rule.

## 4. Family–school coordination protocol

1. Develop a shared description using evidence from both settings.
2. Hear the child's account.
3. State one common capability or relationship goal.
4. Divide responsibilities according to authority and resources.
5. Set communication and privacy boundaries.
6. Review on a fixed date and revise collectively.

## 6. Critical historical reading

When using the 2003 text, separate:

- durable systems insight;
- historically situated empirical claim;
- category or terminology requiring revision;
- current evidence or policy needed;
- present-day, rights-respecting application.
