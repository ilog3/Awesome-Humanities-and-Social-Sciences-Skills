# 观察资料的收集与分析

## Prepare the observation

Specify the question, setting, times, participants, observer role, access, consent, focus, duration, and recording system. Learn the physical and institutional context. Pilot the schedule and decide what cannot ethically be observed or recorded.

## Observe in layers

1. Broadly map people, space, activity, timing, rules, and routine.
2. Focus on interactions and episodes relevant to the question.
3. Sample contrasts: different lessons, teachers, students, periods, locations, and disruptions.
4. Follow emerging concepts and actively seek disconfirming events.

For classroom observation, record tasks, teacher moves, student responses, peer interaction, participation distribution, materials, transitions, time use, silence, and unexpected events. Do not infer learning directly from visible activity.

## Record

Use jottings during the event when appropriate and expand them promptly. A record should distinguish:

- concrete action and speech;
- setting and sequence;
- participant explanation;
- observer reaction;
- methodological decision;
- analytic inference and question.

Maps, seating charts, event samples, time samples, checklists, audio, and video can supplement narrative notes. Tools change the situation and require consent and data safeguards.

## Reflect and analyze

Ask how the observer's role, expectations, visibility, and relationship shaped the event. Code actions and meanings, compare recurring patterns and exceptions, construct event sequences, and revisit the field. Use colleague observation or participant discussion to challenge interpretation without treating either as final authority.
