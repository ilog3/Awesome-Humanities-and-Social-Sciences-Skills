# 实物资料的收集与分析

Material data include documents, policies, lesson plans, student work, diaries, letters, meeting records, textbooks, photographs, posters, spatial layouts, digital messages, objects, and artifacts produced independently of or during the study.

## Source criticism

For each item record:

- creator, audience, date, place, purpose, and institutional conditions;
- original, copy, excerpt, edited version, or later reconstruction;
- how it was selected, preserved, classified, and accessed;
- what it was designed to do, not only what it says;
- whose practice or voice is absent;
- privacy, ownership, permission, and retention restrictions.

Official documents show authorized representations, not necessarily enacted practice. Student work shows a produced artifact under particular task and assessment conditions, not a transparent window into ability.

## Organize and analyze

Build a catalog with stable ID, metadata, source, consent status, summary, relevant question, codes, and links to field episodes. Analyze form, content, production, circulation, use, revision, and reception. Compare material evidence with interviews and observation; contradictions often reveal policy-practice gaps or different institutional realities.

Do not collect artifacts merely because they are available. Explain their evidentiary role and minimize identifiable or sensitive material.
