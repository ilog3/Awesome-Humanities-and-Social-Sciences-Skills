## Worked mini-example

Raw incident: A novice teacher asks a senior colleague to defend a grading decision in front of parents.

- Concept: borrowing legitimacy.
- Property: visibility, private advice → public endorsement.
- Condition: authority challenged by a high-status audience.
- Action: recruit an institutional ally.
- Consequence: immediate decision stability; possible longer-term dependence.
- Theoretical sample: novices who reverse the decision, act alone, seek peer support, or face private challenges.
- Provisional relation: the more public and status-asymmetric the challenge, the more visible the borrowed legitimacy, unless the novice possesses an alternative credibility resource.
