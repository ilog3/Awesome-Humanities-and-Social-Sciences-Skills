# Writing and Quality

## Write from the analytic story

Use sorted memos and integrative diagrams to build the outline. Introduce the problem and methodological fit, describe iterative collection and analysis, present the central process or explanatory scheme, develop categories with variation and evidence, discuss negative cases, position the contribution, and state boundaries.

Data excerpts should demonstrate concepts and relations. Surround each excerpt with enough context to show why it supports the claim. Avoid quotation chains with little analysis.

## Quality conditions

Evaluate:

- methodological consistency between question, data, analysis, and claim;
- evidence of constant comparison and conceptual development;
- fit between concepts and data;
- category density through properties and dimensions;
- theoretical sampling driven by analysis;
- variation and negative-case testing;
- context and process integration;
- explanatory coherence without forced uniformity;
- creativity, usefulness, resonance, and capacity to stimulate inquiry;
- reflexivity and an auditable memo trail.

Quality is not identical to validity as a checklist and does not require every study to end in formal theory. If the endpoint is rich description, name it accurately and judge it by its purpose.

## Software

Qualitative software can store, retrieve, link, filter, visualize, and back up data, codes, and memos. It cannot perform conceptual comparison, decide theoretical samples, recognize saturation, or write theory. Preserve exports and metadata so the audit trail does not depend on one proprietary tool.
