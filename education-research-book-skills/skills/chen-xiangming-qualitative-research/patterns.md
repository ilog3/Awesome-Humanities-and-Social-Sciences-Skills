## 3. Fieldnote separation

`[OBS]` observed event  
`[SAY]` participant's words  
`[INF]` researcher's inference  
`[EMO]` emotion/bodily response  
`[METH]` methodological decision  
`[THEORY]` conceptual memo  
`[ETHICS]` risk, consent, power, or reciprocity issue

## 4. Interview sequence

`rapport and renewed consent → concrete recent experience → sequence and actors → meaning → comparison/exception → clarification and summary → participant questions → debrief and next use`
