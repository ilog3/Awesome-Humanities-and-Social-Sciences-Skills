# Patterns

## Cultural-configuration map

**When to use:** Several practices appear contradictory.

**How:** Bound the historical group; list practices; identify linked premises; map hierarchy, obligation, audience, desire, discipline, and socialization; seek counterexamples and institutional alternatives.

**Trade-off:** Integration can become overcoherence or national essentialism.

## Obligation ledger

**When to use:** A benefit or relationship carries moral weight beyond exchange value.

**How:** Record giver/receiver, status, kind of on, expected gimu/giri, repayability, audience, timing, sanction, emotion, and refusal.

**Trade-off:** Translation may impose distinctions actors do not use.

## Audience–reputation test

**When to use:** Shame or face is proposed as an explanation.

**How:** Vary audience visibility, status, relationship, publicity, repair opportunity, and sanction; compare conduct across conditions.

**Trade-off:** Audience effects can coexist with internal conviction and material incentives.

## Critical application protocol

**When to use:** Applying a classic national-character claim.

**How:** State wartime provenance → bound case → specify concept → cite evidence → test class/gender/region/history → compare institutional explanation → report counterexamples → calibrate conclusion.

**Trade-off:** The concept may illuminate normative language without predicting individuals.
