# Glossary

- **Chu** — Loyalty duty in the book's obligation system, especially toward sovereign/state; historically situated.
- **Culture at a distance** — Study of a culture without direct fieldwork, using interviews, documents, media, and comparative knowledge.
- **Cultural pattern** — Configuration that links practices and values across domains without requiring identical individuals.
- **Gimu** — Duties represented as unlimited and never fully repayable.
- **Giri** — Socially enforceable, more bounded obligations involving reciprocity and reputation.
- **Giri to one's name** — Obligation to protect or restore reputation.
- **Guilt culture** — Ideal type emphasizing internalized moral judgment; inadequate as an exclusive civilizational category.
- **Hierarchy / proper place** — Ranked relational order specifying appropriate conduct by position.
- **Ko** — Filial obligation in the book's differentiated duty scheme.
- **Meiji transformation** — State-building and modernization analyzed as institutional change articulated through hierarchy.
- **Muga** — Ideal of action without obstructive self-consciousness in the book's discussion of discipline.
- **Ninjo / human feelings** — Desires and pleasures treated as human and conditionally legitimate.
- **On** — Received benefit and associated indebtedness.
- **Shame culture** — Ideal type emphasizing external evaluation and reputation; use as a limited hypothesis, not a national essence.
- **Socialization discontinuity** — Proposed shift from early indulgence to later discipline and public evaluation.
