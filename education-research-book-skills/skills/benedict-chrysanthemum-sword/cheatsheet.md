# Decision Cheatsheet

| If you see… | Ask… | Avoid… |
|---|---|---|
| Contradictory conduct | Do different domains share a patterned logic? | Calling it a national personality |
| Formal deference | What rank, institution, and alternatives apply? | Equating deference with inner obedience |
| A consequential favor | What on and repayment expectations are invoked? | Translating all reciprocity as giri |
| Duty language | Is it represented as unlimited gimu or bounded obligation? | Flattening every duty into loyalty |
| Reputation repair | Who is the audience and sanction? | Assuming shame excludes guilt |
| Rapid behavioral reversal | What authority and conditions legitimated change? | Treating flexibility as timeless culture |
| Childrearing explanation | What developmental and comparative evidence exists? | Inferring causality from resemblance |
| Successful prediction | Which claim was supported, and what rivals remain? | Validating the full cultural model |
| A claim about “the Japanese” | Which period, class, gender, region, and institution? | Population-wide essence |

## Mandatory limits

- Wartime commission: 1944; publication: 1946.
- No fieldwork in Japan for this project.
- Evidence included U.S.-based interviews, texts, films, and prior studies.
- Contemporary application requires new evidence.
- Report Japanese and later scholarly criticism when evaluating the book.
