# Chapter 4: 三维叙事探究空间

## Core idea

Every narrative inquiry can be located through temporality, sociality, and place. These dimensions give the researcher a practical grammar for composing questions, field texts, and research texts.

## Three dimensions

### Temporality: backward and forward

Trace earlier experiences, the unfolding present, and imagined or feared futures. Dates alone are insufficient; examine how the past is carried into the present and how future possibility shapes current action.

### Sociality: inward and outward

Move inward toward feelings, hopes, moral dispositions, aesthetic responses, and personal conditions; move outward toward relationships, institutions, cultural narratives, and material conditions. Both participants' and researchers' sociality matter.

### Place

Locate events in specific physical, social, and institutional places. A classroom, corridor, home, meeting room, or online space organizes who may enter, speak, observe, remember, and act.

## Worked example

To study a novice teacher leaving the profession:

- backward: prior images of good teaching and entry training;
- present: classroom incidents and staff relationships;
- forward: anticipated career and feared repetition;
- inward: shame, commitment, exhaustion, hope;
- outward: assessment policy, mentor relations, timetable, labor conditions;
- place: classroom, staffroom, commute, and family home.

The analytical unit is the movement among these coordinates, not a single exit interview.

## Reflective notes

After each encounter, write one note for each direction. Mark empty dimensions; absence may reveal access limits or an over-narrow puzzle.

## Key takeaways

1. Use the space to locate, not mechanically code, experience.
2. Revisit the same episode from multiple directions.
3. Place is an active condition of experience, not background scenery.
