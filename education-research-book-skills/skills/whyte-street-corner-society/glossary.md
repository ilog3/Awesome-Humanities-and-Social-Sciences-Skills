# Glossary

- **Broker** — Actor connecting groups or institutions and moving information, favors, or resources across boundaries.
- **College boy** — Whyte's historically situated contrast for youth oriented toward education and outside institutions; not a universal type.
- **Corner boy** — Member of a locally rooted peer group centered on street-corner interaction; not synonymous with deviance.
- **Cornerville** — Pseudonym for the studied Italian American neighborhood in Boston's North End.
- **Disclosure** — Communication of researcher identity, purpose, recording, use, and risk; current standards require ongoing and context-sensitive practice.
- **Doc** — Whyte's principal local sponsor, interpreter, collaborator, and leader within the Norton group.
- **Interaction pattern** — Repeated sequence of attention, initiative, influence, support, and response through which structure becomes visible.
- **Key informant** — Knowledgeable and well-positioned participant who aids access and interpretation but offers a situated view.
- **Natural group** — Social group existing independently of the researcher's recruitment categories.
- **Participant observation** — Sustained participation and observation used to understand conduct in its social setting.
- **Political obligation** — Relationship of favors, services, loyalty, access, and expected return linking people to political organization.
- **Reciprocity** — Mutual contribution within field relations; does not by itself settle consent, authorship, or distributive fairness.
- **Relational rank** — Position inferred from recurring influence and deference rather than formal title alone.
- **Sponsor** — Actor who vouches for the researcher and enables entry into a network or setting.
- **Street corner society** — Informal social organization structured through peer groups, leaders, activities, brokers, and institutions.
