# Validity and Research Quality

## Quantitative validity

For causal claims test covariation, temporal precedence, and alternative explanations. Audit single-group threats such as history, maturation, testing, instrumentation, regression, attrition, and ambiguous temporal order; multi-group threats such as selection and differential attrition. Also examine population, ecological, temporal, treatment-variation, outcome, construct, and statistical-conclusion validity.

Validity belongs to an inference and use under conditions—not permanently to a design or instrument.

## Qualitative validity or credibility

Audit:

- descriptive accuracy;
- fidelity to participant meanings;
- theoretical fit and rival accounts;
- internal explanation within cases/settings;
- transfer beyond them.

Use rich evidence, field duration and variation, reflexivity, negative cases, comparison, participant response, peer challenge, audit trail, and transparent scope. No single technique guarantees credibility.

## Mixed legitimation

In addition to component quality, examine sample integration, inside/participant and outside/researcher perspectives, weakness minimization, sequential dependence, conversion of data forms, paradigm mixing, commensurability, and whether the integrated conclusion is warranted by the actual point of interface.

## Claim ledger

For each claim record type, evidence, design warrant, threat, response, contrary evidence, scope, uncertainty, and what would change the conclusion.
