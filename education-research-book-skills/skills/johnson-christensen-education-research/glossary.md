# Glossary

- **Quantitative research:** Numerical study of variables, distributions, relations, comparisons, or effects.
- **Qualitative research:** Contextual study of meaning, experience, culture, case, process, or history.
- **Mixed research:** Deliberately integrated quantitative and qualitative inquiry.
- **Exploration:** Generation of concepts, patterns, or explanations.
- **Verification:** Testing or challenging an existing prediction or explanation.
- **Construct:** Theoretical attribute or process represented through operations or concepts.
- **Internal validity:** Warrant for a causal explanation within a study.
- **External validity:** Warrant across populations, settings, times, treatments, or outcomes.
- **Credibility:** Defensibility of a qualitative description, interpretation, or explanation.
- **Legitimation:** Quality of mixed-method components and their integrated inference.
- **Regression discontinuity:** Quasi-experiment assigning treatment by a cutoff on a forcing variable.
- **Multiple baseline:** Single-case design staggering intervention across behaviors, settings, or units.
- **Phenomenology:** Inquiry into structures and meanings of lived experience.
- **Grounded theory:** Iterative, comparative development of theory from data.
- **Joint display:** Structured presentation bringing different evidence together for integration.
- **Data transformation:** Conversion of one data form into another for a defined mixed analysis.
